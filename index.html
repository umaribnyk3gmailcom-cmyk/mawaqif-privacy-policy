Mawaqif

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Mawaqif ("the app," "we," "our") is a prayer times and azan companion app. This policy explains what information the app uses, how it's used, and the choices you have. We built Mawaqif to be simple and respectful of your privacy — most of your data never leaves your device.

Information We Use

Mawaqif uses the following information to function:

  • Location data. With your permission, the app uses your device's GPS location (or a manually entered city) to calculate accurate prayer times for your area. If you do not grant location permission, you can manually search for and select a city instead.
  • App preferences. Settings you choose — such as calculation method, madhab, notification preferences, and Salah Focus duration — are saved so the app remembers your choices.
  • Prayer log data. If you use the optional reflection feature, records of which prayers you've marked are stored so you can view your own history over time.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Mawaqif stores your preferences and prayer log data locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not maintain a central database of user identities. Location data is used at the moment it's needed to calculate prayer times and is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Notifications

If you enable prayer notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device at the appropriate prayer times. These notifications are generated on your device and are not routed through an external notification server that collects your activity.

Third-Party Services

Mawaqif does not currently integrate third-party advertising networks or analytics trackers that collect personally identifiable information. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated accordingly, and the change will be reflected in the app's update notes.

Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke location permission at any time through your device's settings; the app will fall back to manual city selection.
  • You can disable notifications at any time in your device's app settings.
  • Since most data is stored locally, uninstalling the app removes your saved preferences and prayer log data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Mawaqif does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above (locally stored preferences and location used solely for prayer time calculation). The app does not include user accounts, social features, or advertising targeted at children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time as the app evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out at umaribnyk3gmail.com@gmail.com.
